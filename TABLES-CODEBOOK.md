# Codebook for Tables A, B, D, E, F, and G

## Reporting window

All generated results cover 2021–2026. Calendar year 2026 is partial as of August 27, 2026.

## Table A1: Faculty data snapshot

Source: `output/faculty_appointments.csv` and `output/faculty_year_panel.csv`.

The table reports appointment type, rank, research area, appointment percentages, tenure-track classifications, departure status, and active years within the reporting window. The `tenure_track_if_ritten_included` field preserves the unresolved alternative classification for John Ritten.

## Tables D1–D4

These are generated by `code/04_publication_tables.R` and were not duplicated in Stage 06. Publication counts use the curated CV-based publication file. Citation counts in D4 use OpenAlex; peer comparison fields remain blank pending an Academic Analytics or equivalent export.

The publication qualification flow reconciles 386 faculty-publication source rows to 265 unique qualifying journal articles. It applies active-affiliation rules, excludes working papers from completed research outputs, reports non-journal scholarly outputs separately, and deduplicates shared DARE articles at the department level. Supporting D4 quality tables report impact-factor summary statistics, citations by publication year, and journals ranked by publication count. Citation totals are cumulative and should not be used to compare recent cohorts directly with older cohorts.

## Table E1: Within-department collaboration

The qualifying-publication universe matches Table D1: unique countable journal articles in the reporting window. A publication is internally collaborative when it has at least two DARE faculty authors. Cross-area publications have DARE authors from more than one research area. Faculty involved is the distinct number of DARE faculty with at least one qualifying publication in that year.

## Table E2: Collaboration across CSU units

The current table reports sponsored-project collaboration in both directions:

- non-DARE investigators on DARE-led projects; and
- rostered DARE faculty serving as PI or co-PI on projects led by another CAS unit.

Project proposals include funded and blank-status records. Blank status means not funded or still in progress. Award counts and dollars use funded records only, with each project counted once. Internal CSU publication counts remain missing because OpenAlex generally identifies Colorado State University but not the internal unit.

## Table E3: External collaboration

Publication partners are external institutions associated with OpenAlex coauthors on qualifying publications. Sponsored-project partners are direct sponsors on funded DARE-led awards. These are different relationship types and are identified in `relationship_basis`. Missing OpenAlex affiliation data should not be interpreted as no external collaboration.

## Table E4: USDA Multistate Research Projects

The current files do not contain reliable project-membership or leadership fields. A schema-valid template and external request are provided.

## Table F1: Faculty research recognitions

Source: `data/awards.xlsx`. The table includes 2021–2026 records classified as Faculty and Research. The source does not identify recipients or research areas. Titles containing “Fellow” are flagged for verification as elected designations; other selection mechanisms remain unspecified.

## Table F2 and graduate committee mentoring

The proposed F2 concerns faculty mentoring and advancement supports, which are not contained in the graduate committee workbook. `table_F2_grad_committee_mentoring_activity.csv` is therefore supplemental evidence of faculty mentoring of graduate students, not a substitute for F2. Memberships are counted as active in a year when their recorded date interval overlaps that calendar year. No student identifier is included, so the table counts committee memberships rather than unique students.

## Tables G1 and G2

Student-coauthored publications use the resolved union rule documented in `CODEBOOK.md`. Student awards come from `data/awards.xlsx`. Current sources do not consistently identify undergraduate versus graduate participation, GRA appointments, student presentations, community-engaged student projects, or resulting placements/outcomes; these fields remain explicitly missing.
