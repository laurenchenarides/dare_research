## Tables B1 and B2

Academic Analytics exports are stored in `academic_analytics/` and processed by `code/08_academic_analytics_peer_comparison.R`. The comparison group contains 36 public land-grant Agricultural Economics units and 745 faculty before the DARE roster adjustment. The DARE Stage 1 unit model excludes Hayley Chouinard, James G. Pritchett, Gregory Merrill Perry, and Kellie J. Enns from the research benchmark, leaving 21 DARE research-active faculty and 741 faculty in the adjusted comparison population.

Table B1 uses the Stage 1 Scholarly Research Index (SRI) for DARE and the full peer export for the remaining units. DARE's filtered SRI is 0.4, tied for rank 4 with a percentile rank of 91.7. Table B2 uses Stage 1 per-faculty values and percentiles for DARE. Peer medians, 75th percentiles, and maxima are calculated from the 36-unit distribution after replacing DARE's unfiltered value with its Stage 1 value.

Unit-level Academic Analytics publication and citation values are authoritative for this benchmark. Faculty-list values must not be summed to recreate unit totals because an internally coauthored work can appear in more than one faculty record. The faculty list is used only to audit inclusion and to verify the four exclusions.

Generated outputs are in `output/academic_analytics/`; figures are in `output/figures/academic_analytics/`. Exact metric-specific coverage periods remain to be retrieved from the Academic Analytics metric-definition or resources screen and are tracked in `EXTERNAL-DATA-REQUESTS.md`.

## Tables D1–D4

The publication tables are generated by `code/04_publication_tables.R` and were not duplicated in Stage 06. Publication counts use the curated CV-based publication file. Citation counts in the department's D4 publication portfolio use the manually verified Google Scholar supplement. `code/08_academic_analytics_peer_comparison.R` separately generates the available Academic Analytics citation indicators for consistent comparison with public land-grant peers; the two citation sources are not combined.

The publication qualification flow reconciles 383 faculty-publication source rows to 264 unique peer-reviewed journal articles. It applies active-affiliation rules, excludes working papers from completed research outputs, reports non-journal scholarly outputs separately, and deduplicates shared DARE articles at the department level. Supporting D4 quality tables report impact-factor summary statistics, manually verified Google Scholar citations by publication year, the publication-weighted average journal impact factor by publication year, and journals ranked by publication count. The weighted journal benchmark is the mean impact factor across publications with an available value, which is equivalent to weighting each journal impact factor by DARE's number of publications in that journal. Google Scholar is the sole citation source in the D4 publication-impact tables. Citation totals are cumulative and should not be compared directly with journal impact factors or used to compare recent cohorts directly with older cohorts. OpenAlex authorship and affiliation data are reserved for collaboration and interdisciplinarity outputs.

Google Scholar counts belong in `data/google_scholar_citations_manual.csv`, with one row per `publication_key`. Stage 04 uses these manually verified counts as the primary citation measure while keeping them separate from OpenAlex. It reads the columns `google_scholar_citations`, `google_scholar_retrieved_date`, `google_scholar_source_url`, and `google_scholar_match_notes`. Generated files in `output/` should not be used for manual entry because they are replaced when the pipeline is rerun.

## Table E1: Within-department collaboration

The qualifying-publication universe matches Table D1: unique countable journal articles in the reporting window. A publication is internally collaborative when it has at least two DARE faculty authors. Cross-area publications have DARE authors from more than one research area. Faculty involved is the distinct number of DARE faculty with at least one qualifying publication in that year.

## Table E2: Collaboration across CSU units

The current table reports sponsored-project collaboration in both directions:

- non-DARE investigators on DARE-led projects; and
- rostered DARE faculty serving as PI or co-PI on projects led by another CAS unit.

Project proposals include funded and blank-status records. Blank status means not funded or still in progress. Award counts and dollars use funded records only, with each project counted once.

Academic Analytics now provides supplemental publication-collaboration records by CSU unit. `code/08_academic_analytics_peer_comparison.R` removes the four faculty excluded from the research benchmark and writes `output/academic_analytics/aa_collaboration_across_CSU_units_filtered.csv`. Its `Co-Authored Articles` values are pairwise faculty-collaborator links, not deduplicated publication counts; an article with several cross-unit coauthors can contribute more than one link. These values must therefore be labeled as collaboration links and not combined with the unique-publication totals from the CV dataset.

## Table E3: External collaboration

Publication partners are external institutions associated with OpenAlex coauthors on qualifying publications. Sponsored-project partners are direct sponsors on funded DARE-led awards. These are different relationship types and are identified in `relationship_basis`. Missing OpenAlex affiliation data should not be interpreted as no external collaboration.

`table_E3_publication_partner_summary.csv` summarizes the publication network overall, by OpenAlex institution type, and by country code. Institution counts are distinct affiliation entities. Coauthored-publication links may count one publication more than once when its coauthors represent multiple external institutions, so those links must not be interpreted as unique publication totals.

Academic Analytics supplies a second, faculty-pair view of external collaboration. The filtered output is `output/academic_analytics/aa_collaboration_external_institutions_filtered.csv`. It identifies 31 external institutions after the four-faculty benchmark exclusion. Its article values are pairwise faculty-collaborator links rather than unique publications, so they are used to rank relationship intensity and are not added to the OpenAlex publication totals.

## Table F1: Faculty research recognitions

Source: `data/awards.xlsx`. The table includes 2021–2026 records classified as Faculty and Research. The source does not identify recipients or research areas. Titles containing “Fellow” are flagged for verification as elected designations; other selection mechanisms remain unspecified.

## Table F2 and graduate committee mentoring

The proposed F2 concerns faculty mentoring and advancement supports, which are not contained in the graduate committee workbook. `table_F2_grad_committee_mentoring_activity.csv` is therefore supplemental evidence of faculty mentoring of graduate students, not a substitute for F2. Memberships are counted as active in a year when their recorded date interval overlaps that calendar year. No student identifier is included, so the table counts committee memberships rather than unique students.

## Tables G1 and G2
